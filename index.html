02About

I'm a graphic designer with three years in the field, working across brand identity, print production, and packaging — with enough digital design experience to make sure a system survives the jump from paper to screen.

My process leans on production knowledge as much as concept: I like knowing how a die-line folds, how a spot colour will actually print, and where a system will break before a client finds out. That's usually where the interesting design decisions live.

03Process

  1. 01

    Brief

    Understand the problem, the audience, and what "done well" actually means for this client before opening any software.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Explore wide, then narrow to two or three directions worth showing — each one a real answer, not a variation on the same idea.

  3. 03

    Craft

    Build out the chosen direction in full — type, colour, grid, and every application it needs to hold up under.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Hand off production-ready files and clear guidelines, so the system still works after I've moved on to the next project.
